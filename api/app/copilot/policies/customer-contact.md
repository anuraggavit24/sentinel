# Contacting the customer

Sentinel Bank fraud operations, internal policy (demo document written for this project).

## How to call
Call only the customer's registered number from our records. Introduce yourself and say you are calling about a payment they started; do not read out the payee or the amount first, ask the customer to describe the payment.

## What to ask
Ask who asked them to make the payment, how they know the payee, and whether anyone is on another call with them right now. Ask whether they installed any app recently at someone's request.

## What never to ask
Never ask for an OTP, PIN, password, passkey or card number, and never ask the customer to install anything. Tell the customer that the bank will never ask for these.

## If the customer insists
If the customer insists on sending the money despite scam signs, explain the pattern calmly and keep the payment held for a second analyst's review. Urgency and secrecy are the two strongest signs that someone is being deceived.
